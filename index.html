Workflow

Press. Pick. Keep sculpting.

A short gesture keeps brush selection inside the viewport, so your hands stay on the sculpt and your attention stays on the form.

Space

Hold Space.

While working in Sculpt Mode, hold Space and Sculpt-Wheel opens directly under your cursor with the current preset ready.

Point to a brush.

Move toward a main brush or one of its grouped variants. The direction stays familiar, making the right tool quick to reach.

Release and sculpt.

Release Space to activate the selected Blender brush. The wheel closes immediately and you continue sculpting without breaking flow.

How it works

Install. Activate. Start sculpting.

Three clear steps take Sculpt-Wheel from a downloaded ZIP to a licensed, Blender-native brush workflow.

01

Install the add-on.

In Blender 4.3 or newer, open Edit > Preferences > Get Extensions. Choose Install from Disk, select the Sculpt-Wheel ZIP without extracting it, then enable the add-on.
